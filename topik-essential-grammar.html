1. Cause & Reason Patterns (원인과 이유)

Questions 1–4 of the TOPIK Reading test constantly evaluate whether candidates can distinguish between subjective causal justifications, objective facts, and negative blame constructions.

Pattern Semantic Register Syntax Constraints TOPIK Typical Domain
-아/어서 Objective, neutral cause Cannot combine with Imperative (-십시오) or Propositive (-ㅂ시다) TOPIK I Reading / Daily Dialogues
-(으)니까 Speaker's discovery or subjective judgment Ideal with Imperative (-세요) and Suggestions (-자) Listening Dialogue Turnarounds
-(으)로 인해(서) Formal, socio-scientific cause Attaches directly to Nouns; used in formal print TOPIK II Writing Item #53 (Charts)
-느라고 Negative personal consequence Subject must remain identical across both clauses; volitional verbs only Reading Cloze Distractor
-는 바람에 Sudden, unexpected negative outcome Second clause must denote an undesirable result Listening Section Short Dialogues
1. -느라고 (Because of doing [X], [Y] suffered) TOPIK Intermediate

Expresses that spending time or physical energy on the first clause caused an unfortunate or unfulfilled outcome in the second clause.

어젯밤에 시험공부를 하느라고 잠을 전혀 못 잤어요.
"Because I was studying for the exam last night, I could not sleep at all."
Exam Trap Alert: Never use -느라고 with non-volitional verbs (e.g., 감기에 걸리느라고 ❌ → 감기에 걸리는 바람에 ⭕). Furthermore, the sentence cannot end with imperative or propositive constructions.
2. -(으)로 인해(서) (Due to / As a consequence of) TOPIK Advanced / Writing #53

A formal written marker indicating the root source of an objective event, economic fluctuation, or sociological phenomenon.

환경오염으로 인해 이상 기후 현상이 심각해지고 있다.
"Due to environmental pollution, abnormal climate phenomena are becoming severe."

2. Contrast & Concession (대조와 양보)

Contrasting patterns represent the single most common grammatical category tested in TOPIK II Reading Questions 1–4, where students must select the syntactically interchangeable partner.

3. -(으)ㄴ/는 반면에 vs. -는 데 반해 (On the other hand) Interchangeable Pair

Explicitly weighs the positive and negative facets of a phenomenon or contrasts two subjects possessing contrary qualities.

이 제품은 성능이 우수한 반면에 가격이 다소 비싸다.
"While this product has superior performance, the price is somewhat expensive."

Exact TOPIK Synonym Match: -(으)ㄴ/는 반면에 ↔ -는 데 반해. On the test, if one appears in the prompt, the other is almost invariably the correct answer choice.

4. -(으)ㅁ에도 불구하고 (Despite / In spite of) TOPIK II Writing Item #54

Conveys that the outcome in clause two occurred despite the adverse condition or contrary reality established in clause one.

수많은 실패에도 불구하고 그는 끊임없이 도전을 이어갔다.
"Despite numerous failures, he continued to challenge himself relentlessly."
5. -(으)ㄹ지라도 / -(으)ㄹ망정 (Even though / Even if) Literary & High-Register Concession

Recognizes the validity of an extreme hypothetical circumstance while adamantly declaring the speaker's unyielding stance in the second clause.

비록 경제적 어려움이 닥칠지라도 꿈을 포기할 수는 없다.
"Even if economic hardships befall me, I cannot abandon my dream."

3. Condition & Assumption (조건과 가정)

6. -기만 하면 (As long as / Whenever) TOPIK I & II Core

Specifies that fulfilling one singular criterion is sufficient for the ensuing state to materialize.

매일 30분씩 꾸준히 연습하기만 하면 누구나 합격할 수 있다.
"As long as one practices steadily for 30 minutes every day, anyone can pass."
7. -(으)려면 (If you intend to do [X]) Goal & Obligation Connector

Contraction of -(으)려고 하면. Sets an intended target in clause one and outlines the prerequisite obligation in clause two (frequently pairing with -아/어야 하다).

한국어능력시험 6급을 취득하려면 고급 어휘를 집중적으로 학습해야 한다.
"If you intend to acquire Level 6 on the TOPIK, you must intensively study advanced vocabulary."
8. -(으)ㄴ/는 한 (As long as / To the extent that) Socio-Legal & Policy Arguments

Sets a continuous conditional boundary. Essential for Writing Question 54 when defining systemic parameters.

정부의 실질적인 지원이 지속되는 한 이 정책은 효과를 발휘할 것이다.
"As long as substantial governmental support continues, this policy will prove effective."

Grammatical Pattern Formality Level Structural Nuance Test Placement
-(으)려고 Informal to Neutral Subjective intent; cannot use future tense in first clause TOPIK I Reading / Listening
-도록 (하다) Formal & Regulatory Indicates purpose, instruction, or psychological threshold Public notices & official instructions

5. Degree, Similarity & Addition (정도와 추가)

9. -(으)ㄹ 뿐만 아니라 (Not only A, but also B) Universal Synonym Target

Adds supportive factual weight to an initial premise. If the first clause describes a positive attribute, the subsequent clause must also reinforce a positive attribute (and vice-versa).

대중교통을 이용하면 요금이 절약될 뿐만 아니라 탄소 배출도 줄일 수 있다.
"Using public transit not only saves travel costs, but also reduces carbon emissions."

Interchangeable Equivalent: -는 데다가 (On top of [X] happening).

10. -(으)ㄹ 정도로 / -(으)ㄹ 지경이다 (To the extent that / On the verge of) Expressive Degree Marker

Draws an vivid, hyperbolic comparison to describe the magnitude of an emotional or physical circumstance.

눈을 제대로 뜰 수 없을 정도로 세찬 눈보라가 몰아쳤다.
"A fierce blizzard raged to the extent that one could not properly open one's eyes."

6. Indirect Speech Transformations (간접화법 완벽 정리)

A mandatory syntactic pillar for TOPIK Intermediate and Advanced levels. Indirect quotations must be correctly conjugated according to whether the cited statement is declarative, interrogative, imperative, or propositive.

7. Probability, Discovery & Regret (추측과 회고)

11. -(으)ㄹ 텐데 (Anticipating that [X], so [Y]) Presupposition & Context Setup

Combines the conjectural future marker -(으)ㄹ 터 with the backgrounding connector -(으)ㄴ/는데. Sets an anticipated state while making a recommendation, inquiry, or contrastive observation.

퇴근 시간이라 지하철이 혼잡할 텐데 조금 일찍 출발합시다.
"Since it is rush hour, the subway will likely be congested, so let us depart a bit early."
12. -(으)ㄹ걸 그랬다 (I should have done [X]) Listening Comprehension Regret Item

A first-person retrospective expression signifying regret over an action not taken. Its counterpart -지 말걸 그랬다 expresses regret over an action that was committed.

출발하기 전에 버스 시간표를 미리 확인해 둘걸 그랬어요.
"I should have checked the bus timetable in advance before setting out."

8. Mandatory Expository Suffixes for TOPIK II Essay (Item 54)

Crucial Examination Scoring Directive: In TOPIK II Writing Item #54, using polite endings like -아/어요 or formal conversational endings like -습니다 incurs a significant structural penalty. Academic discursive writing mandates the objective non-honorific plain form (해라체).
Syntactic Target Grammatical Rule (해라체) Correct Essay Application
Action Verbs (Present) Final vowel: -ㄴ다 / Final consonant: -는다 현대인은 극심한 스트레스를 받는다.
Adjectives (Present) Word stem remains uninflected: -다 사회적 연대감 형성이 대단히 중요하다.
Copula (이다 / 아니다) Final vowel: -다 / Consonant: -이다 이는 명백한 사회적 문제이다.
Universal Law / Axiom -기 마련이다 (Bound to occur) 노력이 결여되면 실패하기 마련이다.
Necessary Resolution -(으)ㄹ 필요가 있다 (Need to) 법률적 제도를 조속히 보완할 필요가 있다.
