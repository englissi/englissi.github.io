TOPIK Grammar & Vocabulary Master

The key grammatical structures tested in TOPIK I & II that frequently appear in modern K-pop songs.

1. -(으)ㄹ까 봐(서) TOPIK II (3~4급)

Used when expressing apprehension, fear, or worry that an unfavorable action or circumstance might occur.

Lyrics Context:
• 숨겨둔 내 떨림을 들킬까 봐
• 네 맘이 나와 다를까 봐
2. -(으)ㄴ/는 척하다 TOPIK II (4급)

Used when a subject pretends to be in a certain state or perform an action that does not match reality.

Lyrics Context:
• 괜히 도도한 척
• 쿨한 척하려고 애써봐도
3. -(으)ㄴ 지 [시간] 되다 TOPIK I/II (2~3급)

Indicates that a certain amount of time has elapsed since an action began. Note the required space before 지.

Lyrics Context:
• 자존심 따윈 버린 지 오래야 이미
4. -아/어 죽겠다 Colloquial

Literally "dying of...", exaggerating extreme emotional or physical discomfort (frustration, tiredness, hunger).

Lyrics Context:
• 답답해 죽겠어 나!
