1. Automated Kiosks, Cafes & Dining (키오스크 & 식당)
Touchscreen kiosks across Korea require clear operational vocabulary for dine-in choices, spice level adjustments, item subtractions, and payment steps.
먹고 갈게요. (meok-go gal-ge-yo) For here / Dining in.
포장해 주세요. (po-jang-hae ju-se-yo) To go / Takeout, please.
덜 맵게 해주실 수 있나요? (deol maep-ge hae-ju-sil su in-na-yo?) Could you please make it less spicy?
안 맵게 해주세요. (an maep-ge hae-ju-se-yo) Please make it non-spicy.
물은 셀프인가요? (mul-eun sel-peu-in-ga-yo?) Is water self-service?
앞치마 하나만 주세요. (ap-chi-ma ha-na-man ju-se-yo) Could I please get an apron?
반찬 좀 더 주실 수 있나요? (ban-chan jom deor ju-sil su in-na-yo?) Could we get some more side dishes?
따뜻한 아메리카노 한 잔 주세요. (tta-tteut-han a-me-ri-ka-no han jan ju-se-yo) One hot Americano, please.
아이스로 변경해 주세요. (a-i-seu-ro byeon-gyeong-hae ju-se-yo) Please change it to iced.
디카페인으로 가능한가요? (di-ka-pe-in-eu-ro ga-neung-han-ga-yo?) Is decaf option available?
텀블러 할인 되나요? (teom-bleo hal-in doe-na-yo?) Do you offer a tumbler personal cup discount?
영수증은 안 주셔도 돼요. (yeong-su-jeung-eun an ju-syeo-do dwae-yo) I don't need a receipt, thank you.
따로 결제할 수 있을까요? (tta-ro gyeol-je-hal su iss-eul-kka-yo?) Can we split the bill / pay separately?
2. Taxis, Subways & Directions (교통 & 길 찾기)
Short, direct imperatives and address signposts for taxi drivers, bus operators, and asking directions around train stations.
서울역으로 가주세요. (seo-ul-yeog-eu-ro ga-ju-se-yo) Please take me to Seoul Station.
인천공항 제1터미널로 가주세요. (in-cheon-gong-hang je-il-teo-mi-neol-lo ga-ju-se-yo) Please take me to Incheon Airport Terminal 1.
여기서 내려주세요. (yeo-gi-seo nae-ryeo-ju-se-yo) Please pull over and let me off here.
신호등 지나서 세워주세요. (sin-ho-deung ji-na-seo se-wo-ju-se-yo) Please stop right past the traffic light.
트렁크 좀 열어주실 수 있나요? (teu-reong-keu jom yeol-eo-ju-sil su in-na-yo?) Could you please open the trunk?
티머니 카드로 결제할게요. (T-money ka-deu-ro gyeol-je-hal-ge-yo) I will pay with my T-money transit card.
이 버스 명동 가나요? (i beo-seu myeong-dong ga-na-yo?) Does this bus go to Myeongdong?
2호선 어디서 타나요? (i-ho-seon eo-di-seo ta-na-yo?) Where can I board Subway Line 2?
몇 번 출구로 나가야 해요? (myeot beon chul-gu-ro na-ga-ya hae-yo?) Which exit number should I head toward?
교통카드 충전은 어디서 하나요? (gyo-tong-ka-deu chung-jeon-eun eo-di-seo ha-na-yo?) Where can I reload my transportation card?
화장실이 어디에 있나요? (hwa-jang-sil-i eo-di-e in-na-yo?) Where is the restroom located?
걸어서 얼마나 걸리나요? (geol-eo-seo eol-ma-na geol-li-na-yo?) How long does it take on foot?
반대 방향으로 잘못 탔어요. (ban-dae bang-hyang-eu-ro jal-mot tat-seo-yo) I boarded the train going in the wrong opposite direction.
3. Allergies & Dietary Restrictions (알레르기 & 식이요구)
Clear, unequivocal phrasing to inform cooks, servers, and staff about severe medical food sensitivities or personal dietary requirements.
땅콩 알레르기가 있어요. (ttang-kong al-le-reu-gi-ga it-seo-yo) I have a severe peanut allergy.
갑각류 알레르기가 심해요. (gap-gak-ryu al-le-reu-gi-ga sim-hae-yo) I have a severe shellfish / crustacean allergy.
유제품을 전혀 못 먹어요. (yu-je-pum-eul jeon-hyeo mot meog-eo-yo) I cannot consume dairy products at all.
오이 빼고 요리해 주세요. (o-i bbae-go yo-ri-hae ju-se-yo) Please cook this without cucumber.
돼지고기 빼주실 수 있나요? (dwae-ji-go-gi bbae-ju-sil su in-na-yo?) Can you please exclude pork?
저는 채식주의자(비건)예요. (jeo-neun chae-sik-ju-ui-ja bi-geon-ye-yo) I am a vegetarian (vegan).
육수가 고기 육수인가요? (yuk-su-ga go-gi yuk-su-in-ga-yo?) Is the broth made from meat broth?
글루텐 프리 메뉴가 있나요? (geul-lu-ten peu-ri me-nyu-ga in-na-yo?) Do you offer any gluten-free menu items?
계란이 들어가나요? (gye-ran-i deul-eo-ga-na-yo?) Does this recipe contain egg?
견과류는 다 빼주세요. (gyeon-gwa-ryu-neun da bbae-ju-se-yo) Please exclude all types of tree nuts.
4. Pharmacy, Clinics & Medical Symptoms (약국 & 병원)
Over-the-counter medicine requests and accurate symptom descriptions when visiting Korean community pharmacies (약국).
두통약 있나요? (du-tong-yak in-na-yo?) Do you have headache pain relief?
종합 감기약 하나 주세요. (jong-hap gam-gi-yak ha-na ju-se-yo) One pack of multi-symptom cold medicine, please.
속이 쓰리고 소화가 안 돼요. (sog-i sseu-ri-go so-hwa-ga an dwae-yo) I have heartburn and indigestion.
열이 나고 목이 아파요. (yeol-i na-go mog-i a-pa-yo) I have a fever and a sore throat.
배탈이 나서 설사를 해요. (bae-tal-i na-seo seol-sa-reul hae-yo) I have an upset stomach and diarrhea.
하루에 몇 번 먹어야 하나요? (ha-ru-e myeot beon meog-eo-ya ha-na-yo?) How many times a day should I take this?
식사 후에 먹나요? (sik-sa hu-e meog-na-yo?) Should I take this after meals?
졸리지 않는 약으로 주세요. (jol-li-ji an-neun yag-eu-ro ju-se-yo) Please give me non-drowsy medicine.
방수 밴드와 소독약 주세요. (bang-su baen-deu-wa so-dok-yak ju-se-yo) Please give me waterproof bandages and antiseptic.
파스 한 통 주세요. (pa-seu han tong ju-se-yo) One pack of pain relief patches (pas), please.
5. Convenience Stores, Marts & Shopping (편의점 & 마트)
Quick daily phrases for requesting shopping bags, using points, checking sizes, and returning retail goods.
종량제 봉투 한 장 주세요. (jong-ryang-je bong-tu han jang ju-se-yo) Please give me one standard municipal trash/waste bag.
쇼핑백 필요 없어요. (syo-ping-baek pil-yo eop-seo-yo) I do not need a shopping bag.
이거 다른 색상 있나요? (i-geo da-reun saek-sang in-na-yo?) Do you have this in a different color?
입어봐도 되나요? (ib-eo-bwa-do dwae-na-yo?) May I try this on in the fitting room?
택스 프리(Tax Free) 가능한가요? (taek-seu peu-ri ga-neung-han-ga-yo?) Can I receive immediate tax refund (Tax Free)?
교환이나 환불 되나요? (gyo-hwan-i-na hwan-bul doe-na-yo?) Can I exchange or get a refund for this?
해외 카드 결제 가능한가요? (hae-oe ka-deu gyeol-je ga-neung-han-ga-yo?) Do you accept international credit cards?
6. Urgent Help & Emergency Hotlines (긴급 상황 & 도움 요청)
Critical expressions for sudden emergencies, asking bystanders for assistance, or requesting English-speaking personnel.
도와주세요! (do-wa-ju-se-yo!) Please help me!
경찰을 불러주세요. (gyeong-chal-eul bul-leo-ju-se-yo) Please call the police (Dial 112).
구급차를 불러주세요. (gu-geup-cha-reul bul-leo-ju-se-yo) Please call an ambulance (Dial 119).
지갑을 잃어버렸어요. (ji-gab-eul il-eo-beo-ryeot-seo-yo) I lost my wallet.
영어로 말씀해 주실 수 있나요? (yeong-eo-ro mal-ssmeum-hae ju-sil su in-na-yo?) Could you please speak in English?
한국어를 조금밖에 못해요. (han-guk-eo-reul jo-geum-bak-e mot-hae-yo) I can only speak a little bit of Korean.
천천히 다시 말씀해 주세요. (cheon-cheon-hi da-si mal-ssmeum-hae ju-se-yo) Could you please say that again slowly?
잠시만 기다려 주세요. (jam-si-man gi-da-ryeo ju-se-yo) Please wait just a moment.
